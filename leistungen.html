What We Do

A complete hotel fitness studio, handled end to end.

From the first site visit to daily operations — three connected building blocks, delivered by one partner.

Building Block 01

Design and Fit-Out

We start with your property, not a catalogue. A site visit and space analysis determine what actually works for your guest profile, floor plan, and brand — then we plan, source, and install.

  • On-site assessment of available space, structural constraints, and guest flow
  • Equipment selection matched to your typical guest — from cardio-focused leisure travelers to serious strength training
  • Interior design aligned with your hotel's brand language, not a generic gym look
  • Sourcing, delivery, and professional installation, coordinated to avoid disrupting operations
Building Block 02

Staff It Right

A studio is only as good as the person running it. Every VITARO location has a dedicated personal trainer — employed and trained by us, not contracted through a staffing agency and not a seasonal hire.

  • Directly employed by VITARO, so quality and conduct are consistent across every property
  • On-site guidance for every fitness level, from a guest's first session to advanced programming
  • Structured class and session schedules that keep the studio active outside peak hours
  • A trainer who represents your hotel, briefed on your service standards
Building Block 03

Operate and Elevate

Once the studio is live, we keep it running and improving — so it stays an asset, not a maintenance item on your facilities list.

  • Equipment maintenance and safety checks on a fixed schedule
  • Ongoing programming — new classes, seasonal formats, guest engagement
  • Regular reporting so you always know how the studio is being used
  • Continuous improvement as part of your amenity mix, not a one-time delivery
