The revenue logic

How a studio pays for itself.

Five inputs, one line: the method we use in every feasibility check. Every input is property-specific, so we calculate it with you rather than publish a generic number.

  1. 1

    Guests in house

    Rooms × occupancy × guests per room gives the nightly audience for the studio.

  2. 2

    Uptake

    The share of guests who book a session or package — driven by visibility, trainer presence and your guest profile.

  3. 3

    Sessions and price

    Sessions per month × average price per session or package.

  4. 4

    Studio revenue

    The monthly top line the studio generates on your property.

  5. 5

    Your share

    Split according to the model you choose — with staff, equipment and maintenance already on our side of the line.

Illustrative method; every input is property-specific and set jointly in the feasibility check.

Common Questions

What hotel operators usually ask us.

How much space do we need?
It depends on your guest volume and the equipment mix, but we've designed functional studios in modest footprints. We assess your actual available space during the site visit rather than asking you to guess in advance.
What does it cost?
It depends on the model and your property. We're deliberately not publishing fixed prices here because our business model is still being finalized and a number without context would be misleading — we discuss real figures once we understand your property.
Who is liable for injuries or accidents in the studio?
Liability and insurance responsibilities are defined in the partnership agreement before launch, matched to the model you choose. We'll walk through this explicitly as part of the proposal, not leave it as fine print.
Who manages the personal trainer day to day?
The trainer is employed, trained, and managed by VITARO — not your HR department. They report into our operations structure while representing your hotel's service standards on-site.
What's the contract term, and what if it doesn't work out?
Terms are discussed and agreed per property as part of the proposal stage — we don't believe in locking hotels into arrangements that aren't working. Exact terms depend on the model chosen.
We're not on Fuerteventura — can we still talk?
Yes. Fuerteventura is our pilot market, but we're planning expansion across the Canary Islands and into further vacation regions. Early conversations with hotels outside the pilot region are welcome.
