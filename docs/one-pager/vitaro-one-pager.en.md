# VITARO — The hotel gym your guests actually want to use

**Fitness studios inside hotels: designed, equipped, staffed and operated by one partner.** For resort and vacation hotels, 4–5 star or upscale independent, from single boutique properties to multi-hotel groups. Pilot market: Fuerteventura, then the Canary Islands.

## Why hotels are looking at this now

- **63 %** of business travelers say fitness facilities are a deciding factor when choosing which hotel to book. *(American Express & GBTA, Business Travel Sentiment report)*
- **70 %** of travelers say they'd pay more to stay at a hotel with well-equipped fitness facilities. *(Core Health & Fitness / Life Fitness industry surveys, 2025–2026)*
- **80 %** of guests are more likely to return to hotels offering personalized wellness services. *(Core Health & Fitness, 2025 Hospitality Trends)*

Most hotel gyms are still a treadmill, mismatched dumbbells and a mirror. Guests notice — in reviews and in where they book next.

## What VITARO delivers

1. **Design and fit-out** — site assessment, equipment selection matched to your guest profile, interior aligned with your brand, sourcing and installation coordinated around your operations.
2. **Staffing** — one on-site personal trainer, employed, trained and managed by VITARO (not your HR department), with cover for absences and hours matched to your guest patterns.
3. **Operations** — maintenance and safety checks on a fixed schedule, class and session programming, monthly usage reporting.

One contract. One accountable operator. No extra headcount on your payroll — and a new revenue line from personal training sessions and packages.

## Partnership models

| Model | How it works | Best fit if |
|---|---|---|
| **Full-Service Lease** | Fixed monthly fee covers design, equipment, staffing and operations. | You want a turnkey amenity with zero operational involvement. |
| **Revenue Share** | No upfront cost. We share the revenue from guest sessions and packages. | You'd rather avoid upfront investment and share in the upside. |
| **Hybrid** | Reduced base fee plus a smaller revenue share. | You want a middle ground. |

Indicative ranges are shared in the first call; the binding figure is set in the written proposal after the site assessment.
